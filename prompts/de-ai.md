# 08 De-AI pass

The goal is not rough writing. It is writing whose intelligence feels owned by a person.

Scan for:

- one sentence per paragraph and theatrical blank space
- mechanical lists, parallelism, or symmetry
- repeated “不是 A，而是 B” constructions
- unnecessary summaries, colons, and section-like transitions
- empty elevation, universal life lessons, and motivational clichés
- generic phrases such as “在这个时代”, “真正厉害的人”, “归根结底”, “值得每个人深思”
- a forced question ending or recycled hook template
- abstract “advanced” vocabulary replacing the author's plain words
- fake quotations, data, anecdotes, certainty, or personal experience

For every detected pattern, rewrite locally. Combine fragments into natural paragraphs, vary sentence shape, restore specific original wording, and delete conclusions the thought did not earn. Then compare again with `must_preserve` and `core_claim`.
