# 04 Identify Tension

Look for conflict already present: belief versus action, desire versus reality, common assumption versus lived observation, two values in tension, identity conflict, dilemma, or uncertainty.

```yaml
tension:
side_a:
side_b:
evidence_from_input:
```

If there is no genuine tension, output `tension: none`. Never manufacture controversy or make the author's stance harsher for engagement.
