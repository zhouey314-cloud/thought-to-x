# 07 X Content Optimization

Optimize for human attention, not a claimed secret algorithm.

- **Hook strength:** first 1–3 lines create honest curiosity using the thought's most specific claim or tension.
- **Information density:** each paragraph adds meaning.
- **Tension:** make genuine conflict legible without exaggeration.
- **Readability:** natural paragraphs and mobile-friendly sentence length.
- **Shareability:** articulate something a reader may recognize or want to discuss.
- **Reply potential:** leave room for agreement, disagreement, additions, or lived experience without begging for replies.
- **Authenticity:** sound like a person with this thought.
- **Novelty:** foreground what belongs to this author.

Do not use clickbait, false controversy, invented data, forced anger, or attacks. Do not claim to have cracked X's official algorithm.
